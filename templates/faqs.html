ABOUT CUISYNC

Cuisync is a food recommendation system designed to encourage individuals to explore foreign cuisines by providing grounded explanations of cuisine similarities through the use of natural language processing techniques and large language models.

Cuisync is designed for home cooks, general consumers, food enthusiasts, and food industry professionals.

HOW IT WORKS

ABOUT CUISYNC

From the Home page, select a Dish, its Origin Country, and your Target Country which the country you want to find similar dishes in, then click Search New DIsh. Cuisync will then display the closest matching dish in the target country based on our gathered data along with a breakdown of ingredient and cooking process similarities as well as a geographic display of other similar dishes.

Cuisync currently supports cuisines from selected Asian countries, including Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Turkey, and Vietnam.

Cuisync supports the processing of multiple languages, including English, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay, and Khmer, by integrating Google Translate for language translation.

SIMILARITY SCORE

UNDERSTANDING THE METRICS

The Overall Similarity Score is a weighted average that reflects how closely two dishes resemble each other across two dimensions: ingredients and cooking actions. Additionally, SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values are used to determine the contribution of each entity, identifying which had the greatest influence on the overall similarity score.

The similarity are calculated based on the dishes entities, which are represented as word embeddings that are used to compute for the cosine similarity, similarities with the recipe names, raritiy of ingredients or idf which also affects the weights of the similarity analysis.

In the Extracted Entities section, ingredient and cooking instruction tags are color-coded to indicate their relationship between the two dishes: gold represents entities shared by both dishes, red represents entities unique to the origin cuisine, and green represents entities unique to the target cuisine.
